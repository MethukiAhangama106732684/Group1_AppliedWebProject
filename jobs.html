  1. Cybersecurity Analyst

    Reference Number: CYB101

    Job Title: Cybersecurity Analyst

    Short Description: We are seeking a Cybersecurity Analyst to monitor security events, investigate potential threats and help protect our clients' systems and online services. The successful candidate will work as part of a collaborative security operations team.

    Salary: AUD $75,000 - $95,000 per year + superannuation

    Reporting Line: Reports to the Security Operations Manager.

    Key Responsibilities

    • Monitor security alerts, logs and network activity for suspicious behaviour.
    • Investigate and document potential security incidents.
    • Assist with vulnerability assessments and security reviews.
    • Maintain accurate incident and investigation records.
    • Escalate significant security events to senior security staff.
    • Contribute to client security reports and recommendations.
    • Stay informed about emerging cybersecurity threats and defensive techniques.

    Essential requirements

    • Diploma, degree or equivalent experience in cybersecurity, information technology or a related field.
    • Understanding of common cybersecurity concepts and threats.
    • Knowledge of networking fundamentals, including TCP/IP, DNS and HTTP.
    • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
    • Good written and verbal communication skills.
    • Ability to work effectively within a team.

    Preferable Requirements

    • Experience with SIEM or security monitoring platforms.
    • Familiarity with vulnerability-scanning tools.
    • Understanding of security frameworks such as the NIST Cybersecurity Framework.
    • Relevant cybersecurity certification such as CompTIA Security+.
